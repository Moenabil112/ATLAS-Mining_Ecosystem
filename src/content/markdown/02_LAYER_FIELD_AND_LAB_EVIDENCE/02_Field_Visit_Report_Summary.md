# Field Visit Report Summary

## Source

Morocco Isseksi Concession Report, 12–28 May 2025.

---

## Key points

- Site inspection covered Isseksi and other license areas.
- Isseksi is approximately 30 km from Beni Mellal city center.
- Site access includes asphalt and dirt-road sections.
- Grid power was observed in the vicinity.
- Trenching works were completed.
- Total trench length reached approximately 232 m during the reported campaign.
- 29 samples were prepared for AFRILAB submission.
- IP geophysics was recommended to define depth and continuity of mineralization.
- Drilling contractors were approached for DD / RC quotations.
