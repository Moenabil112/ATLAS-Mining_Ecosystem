# Disclosure Rules

## General rules

- Do not disclose sensitive coordinates publicly.
- Do not present preliminary estimates as certified resources.
- Do not present selected high-grade assays as average grade.
- Do not present CAPEX as final.
- Do not disclose internal negotiation strategy.
- Always use NDA before technical detail disclosure.

## Claims language

Use “preliminary,” “orientation-study,” “subject to validation,” and “requires technical review.”
