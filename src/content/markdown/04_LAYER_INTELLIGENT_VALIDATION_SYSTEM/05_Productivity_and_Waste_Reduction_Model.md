# Productivity and Waste Reduction Model

## Purpose

Explain how the digital system creates economic value.

---

## Value mechanisms

### 1. Better targeting

The system ranks areas before spending on trenching, geophysics, or drilling.

### 2. Less blind exploration

Blind zones are flagged before money is spent.

### 3. Selective pilot feed

High-confidence material is separated from low-confidence material.

### 4. CAPEX protection

The system supports pilot-first decisions before industrial plant commitment.

### 5. Stronger investor confidence

Data is organized, traceable, and easier to review.

### 6. Operating discipline

The project becomes a controlled operating platform, not a random exploration campaign.
