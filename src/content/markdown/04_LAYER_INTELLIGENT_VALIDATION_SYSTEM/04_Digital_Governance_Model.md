# Digital Governance Model

## Purpose

Define how project data is governed.

---

## Governance rules

1. Every document has an access level.
2. Every claim has an evidence level.
3. Every number has a maturity label.
4. Every investor view is controlled.
5. Every update must identify its source.
6. Every technical decision must have a decision gate.
7. The digital system remains separate from entry fee.

---

## Access levels

- Public teaser
- Qualified investor/operator
- NDA review
- Restricted technical
- Legal review
- Internal Atlas only

---

## Approval flow

Draft → Internal review → Technical review → Commercial review → Approved for controlled disclosure
