# Regulatory and Environmental Reference

## Purpose

This file summarizes the regulatory and environmental position of the Isseksi copper project.

---

## Key reference

The project is linked to an environmental approval reference:

- **Environmental Approval No. CRI/24/102**

This approval is a strategic strength, but it is not a substitute for final operating compliance.

---

## Required review areas

Before pilot or operational activity, the following must be reviewed:

- environmental impact obligations;
- monitoring program requirements;
- water extraction and borehole permissions;
- tailings storage design;
- land access;
- health and safety;
- waste and residue management;
- community and local authority engagement;
- power connection approvals;
- site construction permits.

---

## Investor language

Use:

**The project benefits from an environmental approval reference, while all operational, water, tailings, and site-construction activities remain subject to detailed compliance review and permitting requirements.**
