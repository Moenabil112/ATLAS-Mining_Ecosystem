# License Risk and Disclosure Control

## Purpose

Define how license information should be shared.

---

## Access levels

| Level | Audience | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Public summary | General external communication | Project location, license reference at high level |
| Qualified review | Screened operator/investor | License overview, validity, area, opportunity logic |
| NDA technical review | Serious operator/investor | License copy, coordinates, environmental reference |
| Restricted legal review | Counsel / transaction party | Full legal documents, holder details, obligations |
| Internal Atlas control | Atlas only | Strategy, negotiation position, sensitive issues |

---

## Risk controls

- Do not imply license sale unless contractually intended.
- Do not expose sensitive coordinates unnecessarily.
- Do not state legal finality without counsel review.
- Do not connect third-party access to ownership rights.
