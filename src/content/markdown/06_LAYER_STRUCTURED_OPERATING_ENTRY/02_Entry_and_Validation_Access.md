# Entry & Validation Access

## Purpose

Define what the incoming qualified operator/investor receives.

---

## Access includes

- controlled document review;
- license opportunity review;
- field evidence review;
- assay evidence review;
- A.I.P.S study review;
- site review pathway;
- validation participation;
- negotiation pathway.

---

## Access does not include

- license ownership;
- automatic production rights;
- equity;
- refundable deposit;
- guaranteed operating agreement;
- automatic access to the digital system.
