# Long-Term Operating Agreement Pathway

## Purpose

Define the possible future operating structure after pilot validation.

---

## Agreement elements

- operating scope;
- equipment responsibilities;
- operating costs;
- production measurement;
- product sale mechanism;
- revenue/profit/production sharing;
- environmental and safety obligations;
- data governance;
- expansion rights;
- termination;
- dispute resolution.

---

## Strategic principle

Atlas Mining should enter long-term operations only after the license value has been strengthened through evidence, pilot validation, and commercial discipline.
