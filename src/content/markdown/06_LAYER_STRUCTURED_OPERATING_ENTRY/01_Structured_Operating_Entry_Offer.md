# Layer 06 — Structured Operating Entry Offer

## Purpose

This layer defines Atlas Mining's commercial offer for qualified operators or investors.

---

## Core offer

Atlas Mining offers structured entry into a licensed copper asset supported by:

- exploitation license;
- field evidence;
- AFRILAB assays;
- A.I.P.S orientation study;
- 180-day feasibility upgrade;
- optional independent digital validation system.

---

## Commercial principle

The offer is not a license sale. It is a structured entry into validation and operating negotiation.
