# Pilot Operating Pathway

## Purpose

Define the route from validation to pilot.

---

## Pilot conditions

Pilot should begin only after:

- priority zones are identified;
- representative sampling is completed;
- geophysics is interpreted;
- pilot feed is defined;
- ore type is understood;
- metallurgical testing is planned;
- environmental and safety controls are reviewed.

---

## Pilot scope

- selective extraction;
- bulk sample;
- processing test;
- product quality test;
- measurement and traceability;
- cost review;
- go / scale / redesign decision.
