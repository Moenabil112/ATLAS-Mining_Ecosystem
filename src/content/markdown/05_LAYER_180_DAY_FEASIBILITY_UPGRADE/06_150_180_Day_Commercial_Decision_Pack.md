# 150–180 Day Commercial Decision Pack

## Objective

Convert technical validation into a commercial decision.

## Tasks

- update study findings;
- correct assumptions;
- prepare investor/operator pack;
- prepare pilot operating term sheet;
- prepare long-term agreement framework;
- prepare board memo;
- update data room;
- define next negotiation step.

## Outputs

- 180-Day Feasibility Upgrade Report
- Investor / Operator Decision Pack
- Pilot Operating Term Sheet
- Long-Term Operating Agreement Framework
- Atlas Board Decision Memo
