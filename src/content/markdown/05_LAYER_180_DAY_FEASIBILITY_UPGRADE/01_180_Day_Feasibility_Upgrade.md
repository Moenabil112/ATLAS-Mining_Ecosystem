# Layer 05 — 180-Day Feasibility Upgrade

## Purpose

The 180-day feasibility upgrade converts the preliminary orientation study and available evidence into a structured commercial decision.

---

## Roadmap

| Phase | Days | Focus | Main output |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Phase 1 | 0–30 | Data and assumptions | Registers and validation plan |
| Phase 2 | 30–60 | Target mapping | Target and blind-zone maps |
| Phase 3 | 60–90 | Geophysics and geochemistry | Updated target model |
| Phase 4 | 90–120 | Pilot feed | Pilot feed definition |
| Phase 5 | 120–150 | Process, water, power, CAPEX | Engineering review memos |
| Phase 6 | 150–180 | Commercial decision | Investor/operator decision pack |

---

## Goal

By Day 180, Atlas should be able to decide whether to proceed to:

- pilot operating agreement;
- staged production;
- further feasibility upgrade;
- industrial plant development discussion;
- hold/redesign.
