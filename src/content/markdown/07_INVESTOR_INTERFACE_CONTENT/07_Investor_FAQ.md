# Investor FAQ

## Is this a license sale?

No. The standard offer is structured operating entry and validation access, not a license sale.

## What is the USD 150,000?

It is a Non-Refundable Entry Fee paid to Atlas Mining for structured access to the opportunity and validation pathway.

## Are reserves confirmed?

No. Current figures include preliminary orientation-study estimates and selected assay evidence. Resource/reserve claims require further validation.

## What is the A.I.P.S study?

It is a preliminary orientation study for mine construction and processing. It provides a base case but requires feasibility upgrade.

## What is the digital system?

It is an independent decision-support layer that helps organize evidence, reduce blind exploration, and support targeting and pilot-feed definition.

## What happens after 180 days?

Atlas and the qualified operator/investor may decide whether to proceed to pilot, redesign, continue validation, or negotiate a long-term operating agreement.
