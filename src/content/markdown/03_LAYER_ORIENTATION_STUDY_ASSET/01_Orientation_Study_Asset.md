# Layer 03 — Orientation Study Asset

## Purpose

This layer positions the A.I.P.S preliminary orientation study as a strategic asset for Atlas Mining.

The study is not a final feasibility study. It is a preliminary industrial development framework that helps Atlas present a larger and more structured project opportunity.

---

## Core message

**The A.I.P.S orientation study gives Atlas Mining a preliminary mine-construction and processing scenario. It establishes a base case that can be upgraded, corrected, validated, and expanded through the 180-day feasibility process.**

---

## Strategic value

The study adds value because it includes:

- resource hypothesis;
- mine production concept;
- processing plant scenario;
- process flowsheet;
- water and power assumptions;
- tailings concept;
- preliminary CAPEX;
- production schedule;
- indicative financial upside.

---

## Correct treatment

Use the study as:

- preliminary orientation asset;
- base engineering hypothesis;
- industrial development starting point;
- feasibility upgrade input.

Do not use it as:

- final feasibility study;
- reserve statement;
- bankable CAPEX;
- final operating plan.
