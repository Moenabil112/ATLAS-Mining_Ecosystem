# Base Case, Upside Case and Expansion Case

## Base Case

Based on A.I.P.S orientation study:

- 2.3 Mt preliminary estimate;
- 1.8% Cu preliminary average grade;
- 800 t/day sulfide ore processing scenario;
- 124.4M MAD HT preliminary CAPEX;
- flotation process route;
- mine + plant + infrastructure development concept.

## Upside Case

Beyond the A.I.P.S base case:

- larger mineralized system potential;
- possible extension of copper mineralization;
- possible associated zinc or multi-metal value;
- additional zones beyond initial study assumptions;
- larger production scale after validation.

Language must remain:

**Subject to geophysics, drilling, resource validation, metallurgy, and engineering review.**

## Expansion Case

After validation:

- selective pilot;
- modular processing;
- staged expansion;
- full plant decision;
- multi-zone exploitation;
- long-term operating agreement;
- digital governance and data-room continuity.
