# A.I.P.S Study Executive Digest

## Study title

**Etude d’orientation pour la construction du Projet de la Mine Isseksi — Licence d’exploitation N°353294**

## Prepared by

Africa Industrial Power Systems (A.I.P.S.)

## Study purpose

The study aims to support project construction orientation by reviewing:

1. geological resources;
2. optimal production capacity;
3. ore extraction method;
4. ore processing route;
5. investment estimate;
6. operating cost estimate;
7. gains and return on investment;
8. project implementation planning.

---

## Key study scenario

- 4 open-pit quarries.
- 200 t/day per pit.
- 800 t/day sulfide ore processing plant.
- crushing, grinding, flotation, thickening, filtration.
- tailings storage with water recovery.
- water from boreholes.
- power from ONEE 22KV network.
- preliminary CAPEX: 124.4M MAD HT.

---

## Correct use

This study should be presented as a preliminary orientation study that provides a base case for further validation.
