# Process Flowsheet and Infrastructure

## Proposed process route

The A.I.P.S study proposes:

1. primary crushing;
2. secondary and tertiary crushing to 10 mm;
3. ball milling to d80 120 microns;
4. flotation of sulfide ore;
5. detalcage;
6. roughing;
7. scavenging;
8. re-cleaning;
9. concentrate thickening;
10. concentrate filtration;
11. tailings dam with water recovery.

---

## Infrastructure assumptions

- Tailings storage dam: 3 hectares.
- Tailings dam located around 2 km from plant in the study concept.
- Process water supplied by 4 boreholes.
- Total water pumping: approximately 1,200 m³/day.
- Each borehole: approximately 4 L/s.
- Fresh water ratio: approximately 1.32 m³/t ROM.
- Water recycling from tailings: approximately 40%.
- Power connection: ONEE 22KV.
- Transformer reference: 630–800KVA in study references.
