## Base Case language

Use:

- Preliminary orientation case
- Initial industrial scenario
- Base engineering hypothesis
- Subject to feasibility upgrade
- Requires validation

Do not use:

- final feasibility
- certified reserves
- proven economics

---

## Upside Case language

Use:

- Potential upside beyond the preliminary orientation case
- Expansion potential subject to drilling and resource validation
- Larger mineralized system hypothesis
- Possible scale-up case after 180-day validation

Do not use:

- confirmed larger reserve
- guaranteed resource growth
- proven expansion

---

## CAPEX language

Use:

**The 124.4M MAD HT CAPEX figure should be presented as the preliminary CAPEX framework stated in the A.I.P.S orientation study. It is not the final capital cost, and it should not be treated as the full development ceiling.**

---

## Financial language

A.I.P.S gain figures must be treated as preliminary model outputs requiring correction and update.

Do not present annual gains as bankable returns.
