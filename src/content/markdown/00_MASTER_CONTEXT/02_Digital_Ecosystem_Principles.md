# Digital Ecosystem Principles

## Purpose

This file defines how the ATLAS Isseksi Copper Digital Ecosystem should behave.

---

## Principle 1 — Evidence before claim

Every commercial, technical, and financial statement must be connected to an evidence category.

## Principle 2 — Big numbers with maturity labels

The project should use strong, large-scale language where justified, but every number must be labeled as:

- documented;
- preliminary;
- indicative;
- orientation-study assumption;
- upside scenario;
- requires validation.

## Principle 3 — License is the anchor

All layers must strengthen the position of Atlas Mining as the license-side owner/controller.

## Principle 4 — Digital system supports decisions

The ATLAS Intelligent Copper Targeting System supports decision-making. It does not replace legal review, geologists, mining engineers, metallurgists, laboratories, water specialists, or financial advisors.

## Principle 5 — A.I.P.S study is an asset

The A.I.P.S report is a strategic orientation asset. It provides a preliminary mine-construction and processing scenario, but it must be upgraded before being treated as feasibility-level evidence.

## Principle 6 — 180-day roadmap is the bridge

The 180-day roadmap converts documents and assumptions into a staged decision process.

## Principle 7 — Entry fee is separate from technology

The USD 150,000 Non-Refundable Entry Fee is for Entry & Validation Access to the Atlas opportunity. It is not payment for the digital system.
