## 1. Core logic

This ecosystem treats the Isseksi license as a digitizable mining asset, not merely as a static license or a conventional investment memo.

The project is structured through six connected layers:

1. **Atlas License Position**
2. **Field & Laboratory Evidence**
3. **Orientation Study Asset**
4. **Intelligent Validation System**
5. **180-Day Feasibility Upgrade**
6. **Structured Operating Entry Offer**

The purpose of digitizing these layers is to transform legal, geological, laboratory, engineering, financial, and commercial information into a controlled decision system that can support:

- investor/operator presentation;
- controlled data room access;
- internal Atlas decision-making;
- AI-assisted target selection;
- assumption tracking;
- feasibility upgrade;
- pilot-readiness decision;
- future operating agreement negotiation.

Atlas Mining controls a licensed copper asset in the Isseksi / Beni Mellal-Khénifra context. The asset is supported by:

The ecosystem must present Atlas as the opportunity holder and license-side controller, with the digital layer acting as a value-creation and governance tool.

## 3. Large-number strategy

The A.I.P.S orientation study provides a **base orientation case**, not the ceiling of the asset.

Key base figures include:

- preliminary estimate stated in the study: **2.3 million tonnes at 1.8% Cu**;
- proposed sulfide processing capacity: **800 t/day**;
- proposed production from **4 open-pit quarries**;
- proposed average **200 t/day per pit**;
- preliminary CAPEX estimate: **124.4 million MAD HT**;
- tailings dam concept: **3 hectares**;
- process water concept: **4 boreholes**, approximately **1,200 m³/day** total;
- process fresh water ratio: approximately **1.32 m³/t ROM**;
- water recycling assumption from tailings: approximately **40%**.

However, the ecosystem must also allow an **Upside Case** because the preliminary orientation case may not capture the full geological and commercial potential of the license. Any larger-potential language must remain subject to validation through sampling, geophysics, drilling, mineralogy, metallurgy, resource modeling, water review, power review, and engineering review.

---

## 4. Important separation

The **ATLAS Intelligent Copper Targeting System** is independent from the **Entry & Validation Access** fee.

The USD 150,000 Non-Refundable Entry Fee is paid to Atlas Mining for structured access to the operating opportunity and validation pathway. It is not a technology fee, not a software fee, not a digital-system cost, not a license purchase, and not a production right.

The digital system may be used later under a separate commercial or technical arrangement.
