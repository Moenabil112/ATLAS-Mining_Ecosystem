# 180-Day Roadmap Register

**ATLAS Isseksi Copper Digital Evidence Base** · Evidence Base v1.0 — Phase 1 (0–30 Day Data Control) · Generated 2026-06-10

Layer 05 — the bridge from documents to decision.

---

## PH-1 · Days 0–30 — Data Consolidation & Assumption Control

**Objective:** Convert all existing documents into a structured digital evidence base.

**Activities:** Digitize A.I.P.S study; Build license, evidence & sample/assay registers; Extract assumptions; detect contradictions; Classify claims; build data room base; define disclosure levels

**Outputs:** License Register; Assumption Register; Evidence Matrix; Claims Control Matrix; Data Gap Register; 30-Day Validation Work Plan

**Gates:** GATE-01, GATE-02 · **Owner:** Atlas + digital layer · **Evidence required:** Source documents lodged and indexed

## PH-2 · Days 30–60 — Target Mapping & Sampling Upgrade

**Objective:** Upgrade exploration evidence and define priority copper targets.

**Activities:** GIS mapping of license; Trench & sample mapping; Channel sampling plan; Blind-zone mapping; ore-type classification

**Outputs:** Copper Target Priority Map; Blind-Zone Map; Sampling Upgrade Plan

**Gates:** GATE-03 · **Owner:** Geology team + digital layer · **Evidence required:** Field + assay registers complete

## PH-3 · Days 60–90 — Geophysics & Geochemistry Validation

**Objective:** Test continuity and depth of mineralization; build the short drilling target list.

**Activities:** IP/Resistivity survey plan & execution; Multi-element geochemistry; Interpretation & model update

**Outputs:** Geochemistry Interpretation Memo; IP/Resistivity results; Short Drilling Target List

**Gates:** GATE-04, GATE-05 · **Owner:** Geophysics contractor + Atlas · **Evidence required:** Survey data with QA/QC

## PH-4 · Days 90–120 — Pilot Feed Definition

**Objective:** Define what material can be used for pilot testing.

**Activities:** Targeted short drilling if approved; Integrate geophysics & assays; Classify ore by type & grade; exclude low-confidence material; Select bulk sample locations

**Outputs:** Pilot Feed Definition Memo; Selective Extraction Plan; Ore-Type Decision Matrix; Metallurgical Sample Plan; Pilot Decision Gate Memo

**Gates:** GATE-06 · **Owner:** Mining engineer + metallurgist · **Evidence required:** Drilling + assay integration

## PH-5 · Days 120–150 — Process, Water, Power & CAPEX Review

**Objective:** Review the A.I.P.S processing and infrastructure assumptions against validated data.

**Activities:** Compare 800 t/day plant vs modular pilot; Review flotation & oxide/sulfide/mixed routes; Update water balance; review boreholes & tailings; Review ONEE connection; update CAPEX sensitivity

**Outputs:** Process Route Upgrade Memo; Water & Power Validation Memo; Tailings Risk Note; CAPEX Sensitivity Table; Pilot vs Full Plant Decision Memo

**Gates:** GATE-07, GATE-08, GATE-09 · **Owner:** Process engineer + Atlas board · **Evidence required:** Test work + engineering memos

## PH-6 · Days 150–180 — Commercial Decision & Operating Pathway

**Objective:** Convert technical validation into a commercial decision.

**Activities:** Update study findings; correct assumptions; Prepare investor/operator pack; Prepare pilot operating term sheet; Prepare board memo; update data room

**Outputs:** 180-Day Feasibility Upgrade Report; Investor/Operator Decision Pack; Pilot Operating Term Sheet; Long-Term Operating Agreement Framework; Atlas Board Decision Memo

**Gates:** GATE-10 · **Owner:** Atlas board · **Evidence required:** Full validated package

## Day-180 decision space

Pilot operating agreement · staged production · further feasibility upgrade · industrial plant discussion · hold/redesign.
